Our Repair Services

Comprehensive appliance repairs for homes and businesses across Cape Town and the Western Cape.

🧺

Washing Machine Repair

We repair all types of washing machines — front-load, top-load and washer-dryer combos. Common issues we fix include:

  • Not spinning or draining
  • Leaking water
  • Error codes and electronic faults
  • Bearing and motor problems
  • Door lock and seal replacements

Brands: Samsung, LG, Bosch, Defy, Whirlpool, Hisense, Speed Queen and more.

❄️

Refrigerator & Freezer Repair

When your fridge stops cooling, food safety is at risk. Our technicians restore proper temperature and performance quickly.

  • Not cooling or freezing
  • Compressor and thermostat faults
  • Ice build-up and defrost issues
  • Noisy operation
  • Door seals and lighting

We service domestic and commercial units from all major manufacturers.

🔥

Oven, Stove & Hob Repair

Electric, gas and induction cooking appliances. Accurate diagnosis and lasting repairs.

  • Elements not heating
  • Temperature inaccurate or uneven
  • Ignition and gas issues
  • Control board and timer faults
  • Door seals and hinges
🍽️

Dishwasher Repair

Dirty dishes after a cycle? Unusual noises or water left in the bottom? We fix it.

  • Poor cleaning performance
  • Drainage and pump problems
  • Leaks and door seals
  • Spray arm and filter issues
  • Electronic and sensor faults
💨

Tumble Dryer Repair

Dryers that take forever or stop heating waste time and electricity. We get them working efficiently again.

  • Not heating
  • Long drying times
  • Belt, drum and motor issues
  • Sensor and thermostat faults
  • Ventilation and lint problems
📡

Microwaves, Extractors & Small Appliances

We also handle microwaves, range hoods, hobs and selected small appliances with the same care and professionalism.

  • Microwave not heating or turntable issues
  • Extractor fan motors and controls
  • Induction and ceramic hob repairs
