About Repair Legends

Passionate about fixing appliances and keeping Cape Town homes running smoothly.

🔧

Built on Trust & Expertise

Cape Town Appliance Repair Legends was founded with a simple mission: deliver honest, high-quality appliance repairs that customers can rely on. We understand how disruptive a broken washing machine, fridge or oven can be — that’s why we focus on fast response times, clear communication and lasting repairs.

Our technicians are fully trained, insured and experienced across all major brands. We carry common parts on our vehicles and diagnose problems accurately so you get a proper fix the first time.

  • Honest diagnoses and transparent quotes
  • Quality parts and skilled workmanship
  • Respect for your home and your time
  • 12-month guarantee on all repairs
  • Coverage across greater Cape Town and the Western Cape
